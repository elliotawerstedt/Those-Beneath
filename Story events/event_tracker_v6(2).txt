MERCHANT LADDER (strangeness only ever goes up)
	Level 1 (Day 1): arrives silently, too warmly dressed, watches the bucket, handles fish gently, warm pouch, long shadow
	Level 2 (Day 2): already there first, no breath in the cold, knows your name, cold hands, never eats or drinks, lantern lights itself, something follows the dock
	Level 3 (Day 3): second voice, wet, lake silences for him, you never see how he takes the fish, he stops hiding
	Level 4 (the end): revealed once, then the game stops


MERCHANT SLIPS (in the days, plus an extra pool in script section 12)
	Day 1 (accident): knows the count ("Three."), says "the deep", warns of the edge before you move
	Day 2 (accident): "my--" then "fragile", names a weight before weighing, hums the bucket's tune, answers an unspoken question
	Day 3 (on purpose): "mine", "I have been so patient with you", "It spoils them when you are afraid", "You will understand, afterwards"


PLAYER'S MOTIVE (cautious, curious, a working fisherman; script section 11)
	0 to 24: professional, "a buyer is a buyer"
	25 to 49: cautious and curious, watches the old man and notes where the road is
	50 to 74: curiosity wins, "I have to know", "fishermen fish"
	75 to 99: "it is only the job" becomes the last excuse, repeated
	100: THE HANDS
